## 🌍 Core Thesis: Why "Sovereign AI" Autarky Is Physically Impossible

Every major capital—from Washington and Beijing to Brussels, Tokyo, Riyadh, and New Delhi—has pledged billions toward building a self-contained "domestic AI stack" immune to foreign coercion.

**_The Sovereign Illusion_** demonstrates through geological, electrical, optical, and chemical supply-chain data that complete AI autarky is an engineering impossibility. No single nation owns—or can economically replicate—the ten interdependent tiers required to train and serve frontier computation.

True statecraft in the machine age does not mean isolation; **it means the capacity to act—and exercise leverage—under conditions of inescapable mutual dependence.**

## 🛡️ The Four Operational Sovereignty Invariants

Rather than measuring "sovereignty" by whether a server rack sits inside national borders, *The Sovereign Illusion* evaluates every technology layer through four falsifiable criteria:

1. **Interruption Authority:** *Who holds the legal, cryptographic, or physical authority to sever service, revoke licenses, or halt consumables?*
2. **Autonomous Runway:** *How many hours, weeks, or months can mission-critical operations continue once external telemetry, updates, or spare parts are cut off?*
3. **Switching Friction:** *What does migrating to an alternative architecture require in capital expenditure, transition time, and software re-engineering?*
4. **Governance Jurisdiction:** *Who exercises enforceable domestic jurisdiction over root encryption keys, model weights, safety audits, and citizen data?*
