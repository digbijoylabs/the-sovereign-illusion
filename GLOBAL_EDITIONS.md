## Executive Technical Summaries (10 Languages)

### English
*The Sovereign Illusion* proves through physical supply-chain telemetry—from Spruce Pine high-purity quartz and ASML EUV optics to Korean HBM3e packaging and grid transformers—that total AI autarky is an engineering impossibility. It provides policymakers, strategists, and infrastructure architects with the Four Operational Sovereignty Invariants and an open MCP dependency audit toolkit to govern mutual dependence rather than chasing illusory isolation.

### हिन्दी (Hindi)

### 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese)
